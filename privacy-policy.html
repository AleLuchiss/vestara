Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Vestara Cleaning LLC, operating as Vestara House Cleaning (“Vestara,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects the privacy of our customers, prospective customers, website visitors, and other individuals who interact with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you:

  • Visit vestarahousecleaning.com;
  • Request a cleaning quote;
  • Use our ZenMaid form;
  • Schedule or receive a cleaning service;
  • Call, text, or email us;
  • Interact with our advertising;
  • Submit a review or customer-service request; or
  • Otherwise interact with Vestara.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

A. CONTACT INFORMATION

We may collect:

  • Full name;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Service address;
  • City;
  • State;
  • ZIP code; and
  • Preferred communication method.

B. PROPERTY AND CLEANING INFORMATION

To evaluate and perform a cleaning service, we may collect information such as:

  • Property type;
  • Service address;
  • Approximate square footage;
  • Number of bedrooms;
  • Number and type of bathrooms;
  • Number of levels;
  • Finished basement information;
  • Cleaning frequency;
  • Requested service;
  • Requested date or scheduling preferences;
  • Whether the property is occupied or vacant;
  • Property condition or level of buildup;
  • Date or approximate timing of the last professional cleaning;
  • Pet information;
  • Selected service extras;
  • Areas to prioritize;
  • Areas to avoid;
  • Accessibility information;
  • Parking information;
  • Elevator or stair information; and
  • Other cleaning instructions supplied by you.

C. PROPERTY ACCESS INFORMATION

If you choose to provide it, we may receive:

  • Door access instructions;
  • Lockbox information;
  • Keypad codes;
  • Garage access instructions;
  • Gate instructions;
  • Building-entry instructions;
  • Alarm instructions; or
  • Similar information needed to access the property.

We use this information only for legitimate service and operational purposes.

Customers should provide only the access information reasonably necessary for Vestara to perform the requested service.

D. QUOTE AND APPOINTMENT INFORMATION

We may collect and maintain:

  • Quote requests;
  • Quote history;
  • Appointment history;
  • Services requested;
  • Services completed;
  • Recurring-service frequency;
  • Cancellations;
  • Rescheduling history;
  • Cleaning notes;
  • Service extras;
  • Customer preferences; and
  • Customer-service history.

E. TRANSACTION INFORMATION

We may maintain information related to:

  • Prices;
  • Invoices;
  • Payment status;
  • Discounts;
  • Credits;
  • Refunds;
  • Tips; and
  • Transaction history.

Payment-card information may be collected directly by a third-party payment processor.

Vestara generally does not need to receive or store your complete payment-card number when a third-party payment processor handles the transaction.

F. COMMUNICATION INFORMATION

We may collect and maintain communications including:

  • Emails;
  • SMS or text messages;
  • Telephone-call information;
  • Quote-form submissions;
  • Customer-service requests;
  • Special instructions;
  • Reviews;
  • Complaints;
  • Feedback; and
  • Communication preferences.

G. PHOTOGRAPHS

We may receive or create photographs when reasonably necessary for:

  • Quote evaluation;
  • Service documentation;
  • Before-and-after documentation;
  • Quality assurance;
  • Property-condition documentation;
  • Damage claims;
  • Customer-service matters; or
  • Marketing when permission has been provided.

H. WEBSITE AND DEVICE INFORMATION

When you visit our website, we or our service providers may automatically collect information such as:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device type;
  • Operating system;
  • Approximate location;
  • Referring webpage;
  • Pages viewed;
  • Time and date of visits;
  • Time spent on pages;
  • Links or buttons clicked;
  • Form interactions;
  • Advertising identifiers;
  • Cookie identifiers; and
  • Similar technical or usage information.

I. ADVERTISING AND ATTRIBUTION INFORMATION

When you reach our website through advertising or another referral source, we may receive or process information such as:

  • UTM parameters;
  • Campaign source;
  • Campaign medium;
  • Campaign name;
  • Search or advertising attribution information;
  • Google advertising click identifiers such as GCLID; and
  • Similar attribution identifiers.

This information helps us understand whether our advertising and marketing efforts result in website visits, quote requests, calls, or customers.

2. INFORMATION WE ASK YOU NOT TO PROVIDE

Vestara does not normally need highly sensitive information such as:

  • Social Security numbers;
  • ITINs;
  • Passport numbers;
  • Driver’s license numbers;
  • Immigration documents;
  • Medical records;
  • Health-insurance numbers;
  • Bank-account passwords;
  • Online-account passwords; or
  • Other information unrelated to residential cleaning.

Please do not include this type of information in quote forms or ordinary communications with us unless specifically and legitimately required.

3. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

We may collect personal information:

  • Directly from you;
  • Through vestarahousecleaning.com;
  • Through ZenMaid;
  • Through quote or booking forms;
  • During telephone conversations;
  • Through email;
  • Through SMS messages;
  • During cleaning appointments;
  • Through payment processors;
  • Through customer referrals;
  • Through advertising platforms;
  • Through analytics tools;
  • Through cookies and similar technologies; and
  • From information you voluntarily make available to us.

4. HOW WE USE PERSONAL INFORMATION

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to quote requests;
  • Prepare or evaluate cleaning estimates;
  • Determine whether we serve your location;
  • Recommend an appropriate cleaning service;
  • Schedule appointments;
  • Confirm appointments;
  • Manage recurring services;
  • Communicate arrival windows;
  • Provide access instructions to authorized cleaning personnel;
  • Perform cleaning services;
  • Process approved payments;
  • Issue invoices;
  • Process refunds or credits;
  • Respond to questions;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Investigate service concerns;
  • Investigate damage claims;
  • Maintain business records;
  • Improve our services;
  • Improve our website;
  • Analyze website performance;

  • Prevent fraud or misuse;
  • Maintain security;

  • Comply with legal obligations;
  • Establish or defend legal claims; and
  • Perform other legitimate business activities reasonably related to our residential cleaning services.

5. SERVICE-RELATED EMAIL AND TEXT MESSAGES

If you provide us with an email address or telephone number in connection with a quote request or cleaning service, we may use it for communications reasonably related to that request or service.

These communications may include:

  • Quote responses;
  • Appointment confirmations;
  • Appointment reminders;
  • Scheduling questions;
  • Arrival updates;
  • Access questions;
  • Cleaning updates;
  • Invoice notices;
  • Payment information; and
  • Customer-service communications.

6. MARKETING EMAILS AND TEXT MESSAGES

Marketing communications are treated separately from ordinary service communications.

When required, we will request your consent before sending marketing SMS messages or other communications requiring consent.

Consent to receive marketing communications is not a condition of purchasing cleaning services.

If you opt in to marketing text messages:

  • Message frequency may vary;
  • Message and data rates may apply;
  • You may reply STOP to opt out; and
  • You may use a provided HELP method for assistance.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails through the unsubscribe link or other method provided in the message.

Opting out of marketing does not necessarily prevent service-related communications concerning a quote, confirmed appointment, transaction, invoice, or customer-support issue.

We do not sell your SMS consent or mobile opt-in information to unaffiliated third parties for their own marketing.

Vestara uses ZenMaid to assist with functions such as:

  • Communications;
  • Service information; and
  • Related business administration.

ZenMaid operates under its own privacy and security practices in addition to the obligations that may apply between ZenMaid and Vestara.

  • Measure calls or other customer actions;
  • Determine which advertising campaigns generate results;
  • Diagnose website performance; and
  • Improve our marketing.

These technologies may use cookies, advertising identifiers, IP information, browser information, device information, and website-interaction information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

We retain personal information for only as long as reasonably necessary considering:

  • The purpose for which it was collected;
  • Customer-service needs;
  • Ongoing recurring-service relationships;
  • Transaction records;
  • Accounting requirements;
  • Tax requirements;
  • Insurance requirements;
  • Legal obligations;
  • Dispute resolution;
  • Fraud prevention; and
  • Legitimate business needs.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

We may retain limited information when legally required or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

13. PROPERTY ACCESS INFORMATION

We recognize that keys, codes, alarm information, and other property-access instructions require particular care.

Access information should be made available only to personnel or service providers who reasonably need it for the service.

We will seek to limit retention of access information when it is no longer reasonably needed.

Customers should promptly inform Vestara when an access code or instruction has changed or should no longer be used.

14. DATA SECURITY

Vestara uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures intended to protect personal information appropriate to the nature of the information we maintain.

These measures may include:

  • Password-protected accounts;
  • Limiting access to information;
  • Using established service providers;
  • Keeping systems and software reasonably maintained;
  • Restricting sensitive access information to legitimate operational use; and
  • Removing information that no longer needs to be retained when appropriate.

No electronic system, Internet transmission, or data-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If you believe information you provided to Vestara has been compromised, please contact us promptly.

15. MINNESOTA AND OTHER PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on applicable law and whether the relevant law applies to Vestara or the particular processing activity, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process personal information about you;
  • Access categories of personal information we process;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Request deletion of personal information;
  • Obtain certain personal information in a portable format;
  • Opt out of targeted advertising;
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information;
  • Opt out of certain qualifying profiling;
  • Request information concerning third parties to whom personal information has been disclosed; and
  • Exercise other privacy rights provided by applicable law.

Not every privacy right applies in every circumstance.

Certain information may be exempt from deletion or other requests when retention or processing remains permitted or required by law.
